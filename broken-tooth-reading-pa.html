What to do now

  • Call first so we can guide timing and hold urgent space if available.
  • Do not chew on the broken side.
  • Avoid very hot or very cold foods and drinks.
  • If you find a broken piece, bring it in a clean container.

Pain, sensitivity, or a sharp edge are signs to be seen soon.

What treatment might look like

Depending on the break, treatment may include smoothing, filling, crown planning, or extraction if the tooth is not restorable.

  • Focused exam of the one problem tooth
  • X-ray when needed for diagnosis
  • Written fee before treatment starts
  • No-pressure decision on same-day treatment

When to go to the ER instead

Go to the emergency room if swelling spreads toward your eye or neck, or if you have trouble breathing or swallowing.

For most broken tooth issues without airway symptoms, urgent dental care is usually the right first stop.
