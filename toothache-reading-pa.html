What to do right now

  • Call as early as you can so we can hold a same-day urgent slot if available.
  • Avoid very hot, very cold, and very hard foods on the painful side.
  • If swelling appears, track whether it is getting bigger over hours.
  • Bring your medication list and ID to speed up your visit.

If ibuprofen or acetaminophen no longer controls pain, that is often a sign the tooth needs same-day evaluation.

What your visit looks like

You get a focused urgent visit for the tooth that hurts. No sales pitch and no long treatment plan for unrelated teeth.

  • No-charge look at the painful tooth.
  • Clear explanation of what is causing the pain.
  • Written pricing before treatment starts.
  • You choose whether to treat now or decide later.

When to go to the ER instead

Go to the emergency room now if swelling is spreading toward the eye or down the neck, if breathing or swallowing is hard, or if you cannot open your mouth.

For most tooth pain, urgent dental care is the fastest route to actual tooth treatment.
