Urgent warning signs

If swelling is moving upward toward your eye or downward into your neck, or if you have trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the emergency room now.
  • Rapidly increasing facial swelling
  • Fever with worsening dental pain
  • Trouble opening your mouth
  • Pain not responding to over-the-counter medication

What we do at an urgent visit

We focus first on safety, pain control, and diagnosing the source tooth. Then you get clear options and pricing before treatment.

  • Same-day urgent assessment when possible
  • X-ray and focused exam as needed
  • Treatment options explained in plain language
  • Written fee before any procedure starts

Treating the source tooth is what prevents repeat flare-ups.
